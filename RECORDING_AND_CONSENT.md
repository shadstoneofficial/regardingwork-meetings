# Recording and Consent

Before recording, tell participants what will be captured, why, where it will
be stored, who can access it, and when it will be deleted. Obtain consent when
required by the participants' locations, organization policy, contract, or
meeting platform. Laws vary; this document is operational guidance, not legal
advice.

Suggested notice:

> I would like to record this meeting's audio and create a local transcript.
> The files stay on my Mac and are not uploaded by the recording app. Is
> everyone comfortable proceeding?

Do not start if consent or authority is unclear. Stop immediately if a
participant withdraws consent. Avoid recording sensitive side conversations,
screen-reader audio, passwords, medical/financial information, or unrelated
notifications unless explicitly authorized.

The system track records all Mac playback, not only the meeting app. Use
headphones, Focus mode, and close unrelated media. Headphones also reduce
speaker playback bleeding into the microphone and producing duplicate source
segments.

There is no hidden recording mode. Recording begins only from the visible
menu action, the icon becomes a red record symbol, macOS shows its recording
indicator, and the menu exposes elapsed time and track health.
